# Mission 1 — Unit control

Status: draft. The player commands a handful of units with no base and learns to select, move and fight.

## Teaches

- Selecting a unit, box-selecting a group, issuing move and attack
- Attack-move versus plain move
- Vision and fog of war: the map starts shrouded and reveals as you explore

## Player fantasy

A small, vulnerable detachment picking its way through hostile ground.

## Narrative justification

The detachment is cut off after its position is overrun and has to reach friendly lines. No base explains itself: they are running, not settling.

## Setup

- Starting force: a small squad of one infantry type, plus maybe one light vehicle late in the mission
- Map: a linear route with a few side paths to reward scouting
- Enemies: small patrols the squad can beat if it fights together

## Objectives

1. Reach the first waypoint (movement only)
2. Clear a patrol blocking the route (attack)
3. Reach friendly lines with at least N units alive

## Teaching beats

- **Introduce:** move to a marker with no enemies around
- **Develop:** one patrol, then two, rewarding focus fire
- **Twist:** a patrol arrives from the fog, teaching attack-move and scouting ahead
- **Conclude:** a short run to friendly lines under pressure

## Constraints

No base, no production, no abilities.
