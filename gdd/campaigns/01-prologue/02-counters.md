# Mission 2 — Army control and counters

Status: draft. The player handles a larger mixed army and learns that the right unit against the right target decides fights.

## Teaches

- Control groups and the F1–F3 selection keys
- Counters: Frame (BIO vs MECH) × Armour (Light, Medium, Heavy) against damage types
- Crushing infantry with vehicles
- Garrisoning neutral buildings, and clearing enemy garrisons

## Reinforces

Unit control and attack-move from mission 1.

## Player fantasy

A field commander outmaneuvering a bigger enemy by picking the right fights.

## Narrative justification

The survivors from mission 1 link up with a mixed force that is pinned down. An enemy screen of mixed units blocks the way out; it can only be broken by matching units to threats.

## Setup

- Starting force: two or three unit types with clearly different strengths (say anti-infantry, anti-vehicle, a crusher)
- Map: open ground broken by a town with garrisonable buildings
- Enemies: compositions that punish the wrong matchup, then garrisoned infantry in the town

## Objectives

1. Beat an infantry group (teaches anti-BIO choice or crushing)
2. Beat a vehicle group (teaches anti-MECH)
3. Clear the garrisoned town and hold it until relief arrives

## Teaching beats

- **Introduce:** one pure enemy type at a time, so the counter is obvious
- **Develop:** mixed enemy groups that need split control groups
- **Twist:** enemy infantry garrison the town; the player can garrison their own infantry too
- **Conclude:** hold the town against a mixed counterattack

## Constraints

No base, no production; maybe one reinforcement wave to replace losses.
