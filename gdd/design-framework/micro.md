# Micro-scaled effectiveness

Goal G9 in the [framework](README.md): **many units gain well-tuned, marginal effectiveness
from small micro decisions.** Common in every faction's roster, never on every unit, and
designed thoroughly rather than left to emerge. This is the design space with the most room
for new ideas.

Each pattern below is a decision structure from [decisions](decisions.md) made small enough to
fit inside one unit's moment-to-moment handling.

**How much** a pattern yields to skill, and the knobs that tune it, is
[elasticity](elasticity.md). This note is the catalogue of shapes; that one is the dial.

## Rules

- **The gain is marginal and tuned.** A unit that is not micro'd still does its job.
- **Common per roster, not on every piece.** Each faction should have several such units, drawn from
  more than one pattern below.
- **Attention is per unit, so it favours the smaller army.** Micro that pays per unit is
  affordable with ten units and not with a hundred — an attention-scaled handicap on mass that
  agrees with G7.

## Patterns

Classified by what the micro manipulates, each with the precedent that named it.

| Pattern | The micro | Precedent | Here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coverage** | where the unit stands decides what it protects | Zero Hour Avenger intercepting rockets | Libertarian Point Defense Drone (anti-rocket lazer) |
| **Rotation** | pull a unit out, let it recover, send it back | SC2 roach burrow-healing | stagger blocks healing, so a heal-supported unit is worth rotating out of fire |
| **Aim** | placing slow or area shots, leading targets | Zero Hour Aurora Bomber | manual Bombard shots; projectile lead |
| **Evasion** | moving to deny damage that punishes static targets, at the cost of the unit's own uptime | — | lazer damage over time |
| **Commitment timing** | choosing when a unit stops to channel or deploy | — | Spot; deployment (the Refraction Tank) |
| **Accrual** | earning a charge through activity, spent on the next hit | — | parked: movement-charged attack bonus (below) |
