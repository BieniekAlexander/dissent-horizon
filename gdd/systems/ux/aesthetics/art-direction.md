# Art direction

*Design note for [Dissent Horizon](../../../../CLAUDE.md).* The high-level aesthetic goals the
rest of this folder serves. Decided 2026-10-04.

World-level direction (tone, technology, cultural anchors, and the two world pillars) is owned
by the **straisara** worldbuilding vault (`aesthetics.md`), because other games share this world.
It is repeated here only as far as this game needs it.

## Pillars

1. **Readable first.** Silhouette, then effects, then detail. Fights are decided by matchups,
   so frame (bio/mech) and armour class must read at a glance (see [README](README.md) §Models).
2. **Ideology made visible.** Each faction's look expresses its politics, including the
   contradiction in it. *(world-level)*
3. **A lived-in world under occupation.** Maps hold earlier civilisation: legacy buildings,
   shelters, inhabitants, fusion infrastructure. *(world-level)*
4. **Satisfying to command.** Weight, impact and clear acknowledgement of orders.

## Decisions

- **Tone: satire.** Whimsy and levity, but still gritty. Inspirations: Street Fighter,
  Mega Man X, Dragon Ball Z. *(world-level)*
- **Rendering: stylized**, not realistic.
- **Technology: futurist.** Levels of advancement vary, but everything shown is far beyond the
  current era. *(world-level)*
- **No real-world cultural styling.** Real cultures are ideological foundations only, with little
  to no aesthetic tie-in, and no race in the world is human, so players have no clear group to
  identify with. *(world-level)* This repository names none of those cultures: notes describe the
  ideologies themselves and the factions' own identity.
