# Sanction sanction grid

Commander-level abilities bought with dominion and aimed at the map. **"Sanction" is an
UNLOCK ROUTE, not a kind of thing** — the dominion one. What a cell grants is an ability,
and abilities also arrive free or bought at a structure; those are not sanctions and are
not described here. Whether an ability wears a HUD button is authored on the ability
(`hud_button:`) and is independent of how it was acquired.

| Note | Covers |
|---|---|
| [sanction-grid.md](sanction-grid.md) | the tier/column grid, unlock rules, supersession, fog gating, authoring |
| [payloads.md](payloads.md) | what the cells actually fire, and the effects they apply |
| [off-map-abilities.md](off-map-abilities.md) | abilities whose payload arrives from beyond the play space — the entry rule, the delivery run, parachute descent |

The split is the same one the mechanic itself draws: the **grid** is a technology tree
(macroeconomics), while a **payload** is a combat effect. A new cell is authored in the grid
note; a new effect goes in the payload note.
