# Resources
## Ore
- Create refineries on static ore deposits on the map
## Population
- Economic rubber band
# Dominion
- "super meter", collected in a unique manner per faction
# Map Pieces
## Ore Deposits
- deposited throughout the map in clusters, a la Starcraft
## Buildings
- Pervasive on the map, representing a degree of settlement in the area, usable by players for things like garrisoning, sacrificing, etc.
## Shelters
- unique structure that spawns neutral infantry, which can be capitalized upon by players (captured, recruited, mind-controlled, etc.)
- Few in number on the map, like AOE4 sacred sites
