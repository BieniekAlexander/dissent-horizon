# World Building

This document provides brief overviews of the factions and setting relevant to Dissent Horizon. For deeper lore, refer to the external **world-building knowledge base** (link TBD).

---

## Baladians

Anarchic communities organized at a small scale — think tribal, Arab-influenced collectives defined by a lack of hierarchy. The name derives from *Baladia*, Arabic for "community." Baladian identity is necessarily amorphous: without a governing structure, groups remain on the scale of Dunbar's Number, scavenging most of what they use and relying on agriculture and trade to survive. They are frequent targets of Haustorian subjugation and are armed in part by the Tselerate Coalition. Low-tech, resourceful, and socially stealthy — their primary technological edge is EMP.

## Haustoria

An imperial confederation with Roman and American Dream influences — perpetually expanding, extracting, and growing. *Haustorium* refers to a parasitic root structure, and the faction lives up to the name: Haustoria incorporates other civilizations by force when necessary, maintains a rigid hierarchy with nominal meritocracy, and consumes resources faster than it can replenish them. Their technological specialties include psychic powers and brainwashing, siege warfare, and laser weapons sourced from the Successors of Tenjin. Energy infrastructure includes a small number of hotly contested Dyson Spheres.

## Successors of Tenjin

A technocracy named for Tenjin, the Japanese god of scholarship. The Successors pursue well-being through technological accelerationism — augmenting organisms, including thought-modification for societal harmony. They are unrivaled hackers, make extensive use of biorobotics, and produce chemical weapons as lab byproducts. They are largely parasitic on Warden infrastructure, piggybacking on the Warden's technological advances while the Warden cracks down on their attempts to steal and proprietarize it. They view Baladians as backwards and irrelevant, and are loosely synergistic with Haustoria in resource extraction.

## The Warden

A decentralized libertarian syndicate whose mandate is to police the universe in service of individual sovereignty. Enforcement is carried out by *Ward* machines — specifically programmed toward libertarian ends, and deliberately opaque to one another for security. The Warden treats organic creatures as self-interested by default and intervenes only when their behavior encroaches on others. Technology skews toward precision: nanorobotics, lasers, cryogenics, and minimalist surveillance infrastructure designed for broad lines of sight rather than imposing fortifications.

## Tselerate Coalition

A Marxist revolutionary force — Streltsy-influenced, with obvious Russian aesthetics — whose primary aim is the destruction of Haustorian governance and the establishment of Baladian–Warden harmony. The name evokes both *Strelet* and *accelerate*. The Tselerate frequently recruit Baladians to the cause, maintain proxy bases within their settlements, and rely on signal scrambling to protect Baladians from Warden and Haustori tracking. Their military technology leans toward speed (overclocking-equivalent for physical velocity), advanced napalm, and brutalist utilitarian infrastructure.
